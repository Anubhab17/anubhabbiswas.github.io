**Authors**: Anubhab Biswas, Lorenzo Nespoli, Dario Azzimonti, Lorenzo Zambon, Nicolò Rubattu, Giorgio Corani

BayesReconpy implements methods for probabilistic forecast reconciliation in Python. It
reconciles hierarchies containing real-valued time series, discrete time series and a mixture of
real-valued and discrete time series (mixed hierarchies).
